Everything is read from public Arc RPCs directly in your browser — no backend. A server-side pinger sends one small transaction every 10 minutes to the Pinger contract; each ping records the confirmation latency of the previous one on-chain, and its fee is read from the transaction receipt. Source: github.com/eaxgtfcs-glitch/arc-pulse.
